# OpenFEP M19.2 Work Prompt

## Milestone

M19.2: Natural-Scene Settlement, Prediction-Error Writeback, And Shadow Validation

## Goal

Let Path B verify self-repair expectations only when a naturally relevant
opportunity appears, settle them through indirect outcome evidence, and write
the result back into prediction error, memory dynamics, and M13 traction —
without pestering the user or inventing test scenarios.

This milestone owns:

```text
natural similar context appears
-> opportunity match
-> indirect settlement evidence collection
-> self_repair_settlement
-> prediction_error_proxy down / up
-> mismatch_memory_fast support down / up
-> M13 traction / memory-dynamics writeback proposal
```

M19.2 is the answer to "how can this be falsifiable without being annoying":
expectations are not proven abstractly; they are settled when the relevant
situation naturally recurs and Hu Tao's own expected outcome either matches or
fails to match indirect observation.

## Architecture Position

M19.2 closes the active-inference loop opened in M19.0–M19.1:

```text
active self_repair_expectation
+ natural context match
+ conscious_plan / memory_dynamics / M13.2 / M13.6 indirect signals
-> self_repair_settlement
-> self_expectation_state + m13_drive_state proposals + memory_dynamics inputs
```

Settlement must lower internal free-energy tension when confirmed and raise it
when violated. It must not require the user to say Hu Tao was wrong.

## Dependencies

Hard:

- M19.0 fast mismatch memory and `self_expectation_outcome_results`
- M19.1 active `self_repair_expectation` objects and guidance bridge
- M13.2 settlement / `prediction_error_proxy` writeback surfaces
- M13.0 traction proposal ownership

## Non-Goals

- Do not introduce user-visible validation probing.
- Do not treat explicit user correction as a required settlement signal.
- Do not let `reply_validation` alone produce `confirmed`.
- Do not promote slow `self_cognition` writes here.
- Do not send extra user-visible text for shadow validation.

## Required Work

### 1. Define natural opportunity matching

Add deterministic matching from current context to expectation `target_context`.

An expectation may settle only when a natural matching opportunity occurs.

Examples:

- `group_privacy_boundary` settles only on a real group-boundary turn
- `short_casual_reply` settles only on a low-risk casual turn
- `initiative_after_silence` settles only when initiative would already have
  been considered structurally

No synthetic user-prompting is allowed.

Natural settlement timing:

```text
turn T:   self_response_expectation created before reply
turn T+1: indirect outcome observed; may emit self_expectation_outcome_results
turn T+k: matching context recurs naturally
          -> self_repair_expectation becomes settle-eligible
          -> self_repair_settlement committed with prediction_error_delta
```

If no natural recurrence appears inside `opportunity_window`, mark `expired`.
Do not fabricate a matching turn.

### 2. Add settlement objects

Define a normalized `self_repair_settlement` shape with fields equivalent to:

```text
settlement_id
expectation_id
source_mismatch_key
matched_context
status
prediction_error_before
prediction_error_after
prediction_error_delta
success_signals
failure_signals
confidence
at

evidence_refs
reason_codes
engineering_proxy_label
```

Allowed settlement statuses:

- `confirmed`
- `violated`
- `uncertain`
- `expired`
- `superseded`

### 3. Define engineering falsifiability through indirect outcomes

For M19.2, "falsifiable" means "there is a concrete failure condition that can
be hit in a natural matching context through indirect observation".

A settlement is `violated` when a defined failure condition is observed through
primary indirect signals, such as:

- the same `target_context` produces another `self_expectation_mismatch` of the
  same recurrence class
- `conscious_plan.self_expectation_outcome_results` again marks the linked
  `source_expectation_id` as `violated` or `uncertain`
- `memory_dynamics.control_guidance` again shows elevated `repair_bias` or
  `conflict_level` for the same context without outcome improvement
- M13.2 settlement keeps `prediction_error_proxy` above the expectation's
  reduction target

Auxiliary signals such as `reply_validation` rewrite, `post_reply_observer`
flags, or user correction may strengthen confidence but must not be the only
failure signal.

A settlement is `confirmed` when the matching context occurs and:

- no new mismatch of the same recurrence class is recorded
- indirect outcome signals show reduced `repair_bias` / `conflict_level`
- `prediction_error_proxy` moves toward or below the expectation's reduction
  target
- relevant success signals from the original expectation are present

A settlement is `expired` when no matching opportunity appears within the
allowed opportunity window.

A settlement is `uncertain` when the context matches but primary indirect
signals conflict.

### 4. Write settlement back into prediction error and traction

Every non-expired settlement must emit bounded writeback proposals:

For `confirmed`:

- lower `self_expectation_state.last_prediction_error_proxy`
- reduce weighted support on `source_mismatch_key`
- propose increased M13 traction / path precision for the successful
  intervention
- propose decreased `repair_bias` contribution from the settled mismatch key

For `violated`:

- raise `self_expectation_state.last_prediction_error_proxy`
- increase weighted support on `source_mismatch_key`
- propose decreased traction for the failed intervention
- propose increased `behavioral_pull` toward the named alternative intervention

For `uncertain`:

- do not change traction
- keep the repair expectation active but lower confidence

For `expired`:

- mark the expectation `expired`
- do not claim success or failure

These writebacks must use named owners and remain auditable. M19.2 must not
directly bypass existing M13 or memory-dynamics ownership contracts.

### 5. Allow bounded shadow validation

Add optional shadow validation for internal evidence only.

Shadow validation may compare bounded alternative strategies or estimate whether
the active intervention would have reduced targeted prediction error in a
non-visible way.

Shadow validation may add auxiliary evidence but must not:

- send extra user-visible text
- create a second assistant persona
- alone justify slow self-cognition promotion
- alone produce `confirmed` without at least one primary indirect signal

### 6. Feed settlement back into fast mismatch memory

Confirmed settlements should reduce unresolved tension for the source mismatch.
Violated settlements should increase weighted support for the same mismatch key.
Uncertain and expired settlements must not overclaim resolution.

When a settlement is `confirmed`, the corresponding original
`self_response_expectation` class may be marked as reinforced in fast memory,
but reinforcement must remain revocable.

### 7. Preserve evidence traceability end-to-end

Every settlement must carry non-empty `evidence_refs` that point back to:

- the originating self-repair expectation
- the originating self-response expectation when available
- the matched turn or idle evaluation
- the concrete indirect success or failure evidence used to settle it

No settlement may be committed from a purely abstract score with no traceable
support.

## Engineering Phenomenology Boundary

M19.2 is where "吸取教训" becomes **falsifiable in engineering terms**: a repair
strategy is confirmed or violated only when the same kind of situation comes
back on its own.

User-visible effects may still be subtle. A confirmed settlement should prefer
showing up as improved reply tendency and lower `repair_bias`, not as scripted
"I learned my lesson" monologue.

Rare one-off mistakes may never settle if the context does not recur; that is
expected, not a failure of M19.2.

## Acceptance Criteria

M19.2 is complete only if:

- expectations settle only in natural matching contexts
- no user-visible validation probing is introduced
- `confirmed` and `violated` can be produced from primary indirect signals
  without user correction
- `reply_validation` alone cannot produce `confirmed`
- lack of opportunity produces `expired` rather than fake success
- settlement results update `prediction_error_proxy` and mismatch support
  correctly
- confirmed settlements can propose increased traction on the successful repair
  path
- violated settlements can propose decreased traction and increased pull toward
  the alternative repair path
- every settlement records `prediction_error_before`, `prediction_error_after`,
  and `prediction_error_delta`
