# OpenFEP M17.11 Work Prompt

## Milestone

M17.11: Closed-Loop Field Validation And Ablation Harness

## Goal

Prove, on a held-out fixture corpus rather than single demonstrations, that the
M17.5-M17.10 closed loop actually does what the theory claims:

```text
following -gradient of the field lowers the free-energy surrogate
better than best-single and naive top-k baselines
across many neighborhoods, not one hand-built fixture
```

and that the loop, run over many cycles, drives the surrogate trajectory down
(adaptation), while each component earns its place under ablation.

This milestone exists because M17.9 and M17.10 each validate FE advantage on a
single fixture. A single fixture can be fit to pass its own test. M17.11 turns
the falsifiability standard into a corpus-level, train/test-separated,
per-component result so the field cannot quietly become "a different aggregator
that was tuned to look better."

Status rule:

```text
M17.9 and M17.10 may land provisionally
M17.11 is the held-out validation gate that decides whether their field-effect
claims remain accepted
```

The hard principle is:

```text
the field is justified only if -gradient descent lowers the M17.5 surrogate
on held-out neighborhoods, and ablating it degrades the loop
```

If the harness shows no held-out FE advantage, that is a real and acceptable
finding. The milestone is about measuring honestly, not about manufacturing a
positive result.

## Architecture Position

This milestone is read-mostly. It drives the existing core survival runtime and
the M17.5-M17.10 owners through replay; it must not add a second cognition path.

```text
fixture corpus (train / held-out split)
  -> replay driver over SegmentAgent decision cycles
  -> per-cycle M17.5 FE surrogate + M17.9 field decisions + M17.10 budgets
  -> baseline counterfactuals (best-single, naive top-k, field-off)
  -> ablation matrix (disable one M17 component at a time)
  -> aggregate FE-advantage and trajectory report
```

Recommended owner split:

```text
ReplayDriverOwner        -> deterministic multi-cycle replay over fixtures
BaselineCounterfactualOwner -> best-single / naive-topk / field-off baselines
AblationMatrixOwner      -> toggles one component, reruns, measures delta
FieldValidationReporter  -> aggregate metrics, train/test separation, artifact
```

This milestone adds measurement and toggles only. It must not change the
decision semantics of M17.5-M17.10; it consumes their canonical outputs.

Recommended landing points:

```text
segmentum/evaluation.py                            (replay / metric reuse)
segmentum/counterfactual.py                        (baseline counterfactuals)
segmentum/memory_field.py                          (field-off toggle hook)
segmentum/adaptive_compute.py                      (compute-off / fixed-budget toggle)
segmentum/agent.py                                 (replay entry, read-mostly)
```

If a new owner module is introduced, prefer:

```text
segmentum/m17_11_field_ablation.py
```

## Dependencies

Hard:

- M17.5 canonical FE surrogate (the single outcome quantity used everywhere here)
- M17.6 memory credit, M17.7 reconsolidation gate, M17.8 paths, M17.9 local field,
  M17.10 goal priors / adaptive compute
- existing replay / trajectory telemetry sufficient to measure multi-cycle
  surrogate outcomes in the core survival runtime

Soft:

- Existing `counterfactual.py` and `evaluation.py` harness surfaces
- Existing sleep / replay summaries

Compatibility-sensitive:

- Existing benchmark registry conventions
- Existing trace and audit event readers

## Non-Goals

- Do not add a new cognition path or change M17.5-M17.10 decision semantics.
- Do not let the harness tune parameters on the held-out split.
- Do not claim a true variational free-energy result; the outcome quantity is the
  M17.5 surrogate, and the report must say so.
- Do not let the LLM score, rank, or judge the ablation outcomes.
- Do not report a positive field result computed on the same fixtures used to
  fit field or compute parameters.
- Do not touch Path A or M10.

## Required Work

### 1. Build a deterministic replay corpus with a train/held-out split

Assemble a fixture corpus of decision neighborhoods spanning at least:

```text
clean single-dominant-path neighborhoods       (top-k should already win)
flat / low-contrast neighborhoods               (field may help or not)
internally conflicting neighborhoods            (ridge / saddle structure)
goal-divergent neighborhoods                    (M17.10 prior shaping matters)
```

Rules:

- The corpus must be split into a `train` set (used to fit any thresholds,
  potential shaping, or compute constants) and a `held_out` set (used only to
  report results).
- The split must be fixed and recorded; no fixture may appear in both.
- Replay must be deterministic from the same corpus and seed.
- Any constant tuned in M17.7 / M17.9 / M17.10 that this harness re-fits must be
  fit on `train` only.

Allowed-to-fit on `train` only:

```text
M17.7 surprise thresholds
M17.9 potential shaping weights / field thresholds
M17.10 adaptive compute thresholds / budget constants
```

Not allowed to change during M17.11 fitting:

```text
the canonical M17.5 outcome quantity
baseline definitions
owner semantics of M17.6-M17.10
the decision of which split counts as held_out
```

### 2. Define one outcome quantity and the baselines

The only outcome quantity is the M17.5 FE surrogate (or its bounded delta). Every
metric in this milestone reduces to it.

For each held-out neighborhood, compute the subsequent FE surrogate under:

```text
field_enabled_decision          (full M17.9 / M17.10 loop)
best_single_counterfactual      (strongest single path / memory)
naive_topk_counterfactual       (additive top-k aggregate)
field_off_counterfactual        (coarse retrieval only, no field, no adaptive compute)
```

Required aggregate metrics over the held-out set:

```text
mean_fe_advantage_vs_best_single
mean_fe_advantage_vs_naive_topk
mean_fe_advantage_vs_field_off
median_fe_advantage_vs_best_single
median_fe_advantage_vs_naive_topk
win_rate                         (fraction of neighborhoods where field strictly lowers FE)
no_gain_rate                     (fraction audited as field_divergent_no_gain)
regression_rate                  (fraction where field raised FE vs baseline)
p90_regression_magnitude         (tail-risk size when field loses)
```

`regression_rate` must be reported prominently. A loop that wins on average but
regresses badly on a meaningful slice is not silently acceptable.

At least one paired-distribution summary must be preserved so the report cannot
hide "many tiny wins, a few large losses" behind the mean alone.

### 3. Measure the multi-cycle FE trajectory (adaptation)

Beyond single-decision advantage, run the full loop over multi-cycle replays and
record whether the M15.0 free-energy-proxy trajectory declines as credit (M17.6),
reconsolidation (M17.7), and paths (M17.8) accumulate.

Requirements:

- report the surrogate trajectory slope over cycles with the full loop enabled
- compare against a frozen-memory control where credit / reconsolidation / path
  updates are disabled
- a genuine adaptation claim requires the full loop to bend the trajectory down
  more than the frozen-memory control on held-out cycles

This trajectory comparison is part of the validation gate, not a decorative
appendix. If held-out trajectory improvement is absent, the loop may still ship
as an engineering experiment, but its adaptation claim is not accepted.

### 4. Build a per-component ablation matrix

Toggle exactly one component off at a time and re-measure the held-out metrics:

```text
ablate M17.6 credit assignment
ablate M17.7 surprise-gated reconsolidation
ablate M17.8 path substrate (episode-first fallback)
ablate M17.9 local field (top-k fallback)
ablate M17.10 goal priors
ablate M17.10 adaptive compute (fixed budget)
```

Rules:

- each toggle must be a clean on/off of that owner, not a partial re-tuning
- report the FE-advantage and trajectory delta caused by each ablation
- a component that, when removed, does not degrade any held-out metric must be
  flagged `component_no_measurable_contribution` rather than assumed useful
- ablation must be deterministic and reproducible from the recorded split

### 5. Add an overfitting guard

The report must make the train/held-out separation explicit and machine-checkable:

```text
params_fit_on: train
metrics_reported_on: held_out
fixtures_overlap: false
```

If any reported positive result was computed on fixtures used for fitting, the
harness must refuse to label it a validated result and must emit a
`leakage_detected` audit event.

### 6. Emit a bounded report artifact and audit events

Required event family:

```text
FieldValidationRunStartedEvent
FieldValidationResultEvent
AblationResultEvent
FieldValidationLeakageEvent
```

The result artifact (for example `reports/m17_11_field_validation.md` or a JSON
sibling) must contain:

```text
corpus split summary
held-out FE-advantage metrics (vs best-single, naive top-k, field-off)
paired-distribution summary (including median and tail regression magnitude)
multi-cycle trajectory slopes (full loop vs frozen-memory control)
ablation matrix
overfitting-guard status
an explicit honesty statement: outcome quantity is the M17.5 surrogate, not a
  learned generative-model free energy
```

Do not expose raw prompt text or full memory dumps in the artifact.

## Acceptance Criteria

M17.11 is complete when:

1. A deterministic fixture corpus exists with a fixed, non-overlapping
   train / held-out split.
2. Field-enabled decisions are compared against best-single, naive top-k, and
   field-off baselines using the M17.5 surrogate as the single outcome quantity.
3. Held-out FE-advantage, win rate, no-gain rate, and regression rate are all
   reported, with regression rate made prominent.
4. Median FE advantage and tail-regression magnitude are reported so mean gains
   cannot hide asymmetric losses.
5. The multi-cycle FE trajectory under the full loop is compared against a
   frozen-memory control on held-out cycles.
6. A per-component ablation matrix reports the degradation caused by removing
   each of M17.6, M17.7, M17.8, M17.9, and each M17.10 effect.
7. Any component with no measurable held-out contribution is flagged, not assumed
   useful.
8. The overfitting guard proves params were fit on train and reported on held-out;
   leakage emits an explicit audit event.
9. The artifact carries an honesty statement that the outcome quantity is the
   M17.5 surrogate, not a learned generative-model free energy.
10. M17.9 and M17.10 field-effect claims remain provisional until this held-out
    validation runs; a negative held-out result must not be rewritten into a pass.
11. The harness changes no M17.5-M17.10 decision semantics; it only measures.
12. The implementation is deterministic and replay-safe.
13. Path A and M10 remain untouched.

## Verification Standard

Use the held-out split where:

```text
the corpus contains dominant-path, flat, conflicting, and goal-divergent neighborhoods
params were fit only on the train split
```

Expected:

```text
on conflicting / flat neighborhoods, mean_fe_advantage_vs_naive_topk > 0
  and mean_fe_advantage_vs_best_single > 0 on held-out fixtures
on dominant-path neighborhoods, the field is suppressed or top-k-equivalent
  and does not regress FE
the multi-cycle trajectory under the full loop declines more than the
  frozen-memory control
removing the M17.9 field or M17.6 credit measurably degrades held-out advantage
fixtures_overlap == false and no FieldValidationLeakageEvent is emitted
```

If the held-out FE advantage is absent, the milestone still passes by reporting
that result honestly, and it becomes the evidence that the field layer, as built,
is not yet justified over top-k.
